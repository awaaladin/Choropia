## Brand & Style

This design system targets high-net-worth individuals, institutional collectors, and discerning professionals operating within a high-stakes marketplace. The brand personality communicates unwavering financial rigor, institutional authority, and bespoke digital craftsmanship. The emotional response is absolute trust, quiet sophistication, and surgical precision—reminiscent of premier global private banks and luxury auction houses rather than retail e-commerce.

The design movement adheres to **Corporate Modernism married with High-End Editorial Precision**, native to Material Design 3 (M3). It avoids hyper-saturated gradients, gamified micro-interactions, playful illustrations, or bubbly container forms. Visual hierarchy is established through meticulous typographic contrast, calibrated tonal borders, and razor-sharp component execution.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an asymmetric tonal relationship between abyssal navy and immaculate white, mediated by high-clarity cool architectural slates.

### Light Mode (Default)
- **Surface & Background**: Clean crisp ivory-tinted neutral (`#FFFFFF` to `#F5F7FA`) provides an open, airy gallery aesthetic.
- **Primary & Headings**: Deep Midnight Navy (`#0B192C`) commands maximum structural and typographic weight.
- **Secondary & Structural Accents**: Tailored Cobalt-Slate (`#1E3E62`) anchors primary interactive boundaries, tab active indicators, and secondary metrics.
- **Dividers & Surface Boundaries**: Precision Slate (`#E2E8F0`) defines zero-shadow boundaries.
- **Tertiary / Subdued Text**: Muted Charcoal-Slate (`#334155`) guarantees WCAG AAA compliant readability for descriptions, timestamps, and metadata.

### Dark Mode
- **Surface & Background**: Deep Midnight Slate (`#0B192C` base with `#070F1B` background depth).
- **Surface Containers**: Tiered steps utilizing `#0E223A` (low) to `#132B47` (high).
- **Primary Text**: Crisp Studio White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Dividers & Outlines**: Calibrated deep midnight lines (`#1E3E62` at 40%–60% alpha).
- **Functional Accents**: `#E2E8F0` highlights for active toggle states and high-priority indicators.

## Typography

Typography relies strictly on **Inter** to express architectural and algorithmic accuracy. Monospaced tabular figures (`tnum`, `cv05`) are systematically enabled across numerical components, ensuring monetary values, valuations, and ledger columns align with micro-millimeter precision.

Headlines leverage moderate tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) paired with medium/semi-bold weights (`500`/`600`) to present institutional presence without shouting. Body copy maintains strict vertical cadence, prioritizing clarity over stylistic embellishment. All uppercase labels (e.g., status badges, verification pill headers) utilize expanded letter-spacing (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) in compact weights.

## Layout & Spacing

The architecture operates on an 8pt baseline grid (subdivided into 4pt for micro-alignments such as chip heights, icons, and input padding).

- **Mobile (Compact: 0–599dp)**: 4-column fluid grid, 16dp margins, 12dp gutters. Vertical pacing is strict; actionable rows conform to a standardized 56dp or 64dp touch target.
- **Foldables / Small Tablets (Medium: 600–839dp)**: 8-column fluid grid, 24dp margins, 16dp gutters. Split-pane layout activates for master-detail records (e.g., asset browsing on left pane, inspection dossier on right).
- **Large Tablets / Desktop (Expanded: ≥840dp)**: 12-column grid anchored to a maximum content container width of 1200dp, centered, with 32dp margins and 24dp gutters.

Density is balanced: negative space around high-value transaction cards reinforces confidence, while data tables compress vertically with `space-sm` and `space-md` paddings to retain high information density.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately minimizes standard heavy drop-shadows to distinguish itself from consumer platforms. Visual separation is accomplished using **Surface Tiers** and **Low-Contrast Structural Outlines**.

- **Elevation Level 0 (Base)**: `#F5F7FA` (Light) / `#070F1B` (Dark). Completely flat background plane.
- **Elevation Level 1 (Resting Cards & Surfaces)**: `#FFFFFF` (Light) / `#0B192C` (Dark). Outlined with a continuous 1px stroke of `#E2E8F0` (Light) or `#1E3E62` (Dark at 60% opacity). No drop shadow.
- **Elevation Level 2 (Floating Action Bars, Dropdowns, Sheets)**: Crisp surface background supplemented by a calibrated atmospheric shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0px 4px 16px -2px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.06), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.04)` (Light Mode).
  - In Dark Mode, atmospheric shadows are replaced with an ambient key-line highlight: 1px outline in `#1E3E62` with a subtle upper boundary light of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Elevation Level 3 (Dialogs & Critical Confirmation Modals)**: Scrim overlay at 60% opacity (`#0B192C` with 60% alpha) coupled with a centralized container bordered by a sharp 1px `#CBD5E1` boundary.

## Shapes

The geometric identity is defined by **Soft Crispness (Level 1)**. Rounded corners are restrained, establishing an architectural, engineered contour:

- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Segmented Controls)**: 4px corner radius (`0.25rem`). This avoids both the clinical austerity of raw 0px corners and the playful informality of pill/capsule shapes.
- **Cards, Panels, and Containers**: 8px corner radius (`0.5rem`).
- **Modal Dialogs & Bottom Sheets**: 12px corner radius (`0.75rem`) exclusively on the top vertices for sheets, and all four corners for dialogs.
- **Badges & Micro Status Tags**: 2px corner radius (`0.125rem`), strictly tabular and architectural. Circular avatars remain the sole exception, clipped to 50% circular geometry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Deep Midnight Navy (`#0B192C`) surface with Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) text and icons. Flat, zero shadow, 4px border radius. Height: 48dp on mobile. State layers use 8% white overlay on pressed.
- **Secondary / Outlined**: 1px stroke of `#1E3E62`, transparent background, `#0B192C` text (Light) or `#FFFFFF` (Dark).
- **Text Button**: Minimalist, no container, `#1E3E62` medium weight typography, 8dp horizontal padding.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Height: 32dp. Non-pill form (4px radius).
- **Inactive**: Surface `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, label color `#334155`.
- **Selected**: Surface `#0B192C`, border `#0B192C`, label `#FFFFFF`. Outlined Material Symbol (16dp) prefix.

### Lists & Row Items
- Single and multi-line rows bound by a 1px bottom border (`#E2E8F0` in Light Mode, `#1E3E62` at 40% in Dark Mode).
- Content features tabular alphanumeric readouts aligned flush to the trailing edge. Leading elements are either 20dp hairline outlined Material Symbols or a structured 40dp square asset preview with a 2px radius.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Outlined style only (Material 3 OutlinedTextField).
- 1px border in `#CBD5E1` at rest, transitioning to a crisp 1.5px `#0B192C` border on focus (no glow, no diffuse halos).
- Supporting text and floating label conform strictly to `Inter`, moving to `label-md` on focus with 0.04em tracking.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Square checkbox elements with 2px radius.
- Active check marks utilize `#0B192C` fill with a sharp white glyph.
- Radio circles maintain a 1.5px outer boundary and a sharp 50% centered solid bullet.

### Marketplace Cards
- Structural framing via 1px `#E2E8F0` border on an elevated white canvas. Zero shadow by default; hover/focus states activate Elevation Level 2.
- Media sections inside cards maintain sharp aspect ratios (16:9 or 1:1) with direct 0px radius on top edges conforming to the parent card’s 8px outer shell.
- Includes a dedicated verification ticker badge: `#F5F7FA` container, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `#0B192C` text with a 14dp `verified` outlined icon.

### Iconography
- System-wide deployment of **Material Symbols Outlined** (Stroke weight: 400, Grade: 0, Optical size: 24).
- Icons remain single-color, inheriting the contextual typographic slate/navy color token without fills or dual-tone shading.
