4. How payment and escrow work

  1. The buyer pays the item price (and any delivery fee they agreed to pay) on Choropia. Payments are processed by Paystack. We never see or store your card details.
  2. The money is held in escrow. The item is marked sold so nobody else can buy it. If the item turns out to have been sold to someone else a moment before, the buyer is refunded in full.
  3. When the buyer confirms they have received the item, or when a delivery is completed with the buyer's handover code, the money is released and the seller is paid by bank transfer to the account they added.
  4. If the buyer does nothing after the seller marks the item delivered, the seller is paid automatically after 3 days.
  5. If the seller does not dispatch the item within 7 days of payment, the buyer is refunded automatically and the item goes back on sale.
  6. Only confirm that you have received an item once it is really in your hands.
