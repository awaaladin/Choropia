{{ company }} ("Choropia", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who sees it, how long we keep it and what rights you have under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA). We are the data controller for the data described here.

1. What we collect

  • Account data: your name, email address, phone number (if you give it), password (stored only as a one-way hash), profile photo and bio, and the area you are in.
  • Marketplace activity: listings and photos you post, messages you send in chat, reviews, orders, disputes, notifications and what you watch or follow.
  • Payment data: payments are made through Paystack. We receive the result of the payment, the amount and a reference. We do not receive or store your card number. If you are paid out, we store your bank name, the last four digits of your account number and the account name; the full account number is held by Paystack as a payout recipient.
  • Delivery data: the pickup and delivery area, the delivery address note the buyer writes, the handover code, and a photo taken by the driver as proof of delivery.
  • Driver verification data (drivers only): full name, phone and WhatsApp numbers, vehicle type and plate number, your guarantor's name and phone number, and photographs or copies of your passport photograph, driver licence or national ID, guarantor form and vehicle papers.
  • Technical data: device and app information, IP address and logs needed to keep the service secure and working.

2. Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To create your account, run the marketplace, hold and release payments, and deliver items: to perform our contract with you.
  • To verify drivers and decide whether to approve them: your consent, which you give when you apply and can withdraw (withdrawing means we can no longer keep you as a driver), and our legitimate interest in protecting buyers and sellers from fraud and harm.
  • To prevent fraud, resolve disputes, keep records for tax and financial rules, and respond to lawful requests: legal obligation and legitimate interest.
  • To send you emails and notifications about your account, orders, payments, payouts and deliveries: contract. We do not sell your data and we do not send third-party advertising.

3. Who sees your data

  • Other users: your name and profile, your listings and reviews are public. Your phone number and exact address are never shown to other users before an order is paid. After a driver accepts a paid delivery, that driver sees what they need to do the job: the seller's pickup contact, and the buyer's name, phone number and address note. The buyer and seller see the driver's name, vehicle and phone numbers.
  • Drivers' identity documents are visible only to authorised Choropia staff doing verification, and every time one is opened it is logged. They are never shown to buyers, sellers or other drivers.
  • Service providers that help us run Choropia and only act on our instructions: Paystack (payments and payouts), our email provider (Resend), our hosting and database providers (Vercel and Supabase), our caching provider (Upstash) and file storage.
  • Authorities when the law requires it or to protect people from serious harm.

4. Where your data is processed

Some of our providers store or process data outside Nigeria. When your data leaves Nigeria we rely on the safeguards the NDPA allows, such as contracts with those providers that require them to protect your data to an equivalent standard.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account and marketplace data: while your account is open, and for a reasonable time afterwards for disputes and legal claims.
  • Payment, payout and order records: for as long as financial and tax rules require us to keep them.
  • Driver identity documents: while you are an approved driver. If you are rejected or you leave, we delete them within a reasonable period unless we must keep them to deal with a dispute, fraud or a legal duty.
  • Delivery proof photos: for as long as an order can still be disputed and for a reasonable time afterwards.

6. How we protect it

Passwords are hashed. Connections use HTTPS. Drivers' identity documents are encrypted before they are stored, and the key is kept separately from the database. Staff access is limited and logged. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach puts your rights at risk we will tell you and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within the time the law requires.

7. Your rights

Under the NDPA you can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, have it deleted, limit or object to how we use it, receive it in a portable form, and withdraw consent you gave us. We may keep some data where the law requires it. You also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.
