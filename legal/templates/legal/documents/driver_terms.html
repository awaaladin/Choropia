Once approved, you publish a price list: from one area to another, one fixed price. Sellers choose drivers using these prices, and buyers see the exact price before paying. A price you publish is the price you have agreed to for that route; you cannot ask the customer for more. If you want to change a price, update it on your price list; it does not change jobs already paid for.

4. Taking jobs

  • When a delivery is paid, every driver the seller picked is told. The first to accept gets the job; the others are told it has been taken.
  • The seller can change their mind and give the job to another driver before pickup.
  • Only accept a job you will do. Repeated no-shows, late deliveries or cancellations can lead to suspension.
  • The buyer's exact address and phone number are shown to you only after you accept, and only so you can deliver. Do not keep, share or use them for anything else.

5. Pickup and delivery

  • Pick the item up from the seller, and look after it. Do not open it. Do not carry anything illegal or dangerous.
  • Deliver to the buyer in person. The buyer has a six-digit handover code. Ask for it only when you are handing over the item, and do not accept it from anyone else, by message, or before the item is in their hands.
  • Enter the code and take a clear photo of the delivered item in the app. That completes the delivery and releases the payments. Do not share or ask the buyer to share the code in any other way.
  • If the buyer disputes the order, the money stays in escrow until Choropia has decided.
