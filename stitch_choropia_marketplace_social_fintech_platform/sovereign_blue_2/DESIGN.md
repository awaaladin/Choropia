## Brand & Style

This design system expresses institutional authority, sovereign security, and modern financial empowerment. Positioned at the intersection of high-capital fintech and executive-tier social commerce, the aesthetic balances the gravitas of elite financial institutions with the agility and responsiveness of contemporary digital platforms.

### Aesthetic Philosophy
The visual direction fuses **Corporate Modernism** with **Architectural Minimalism**:
- **Tone:** Authoritative, disciplined, resolute, and clear.
- **Visual Tenor:** Deep obsidian and naval voids paired with laser-sharp white typography, ice-blue structural lines, and muted slate secondary information.
- **Restraint:** Complete omission of playful or cartoonish metaphors, excessive gradient washes, or soft pastel treatments. Every line, corner, and surface communicates permanence, fidelity, and sovereign value.

## Colors

The system uses a calibrated palette anchored in deep naval voids, engineered for high contrast and sustained analytical focus.

### Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#060d17`):** The foundational dark space for the global viewport.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#0b192c`):** Cards, primary navigation panes, and data modules.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#0f2238`):** Elevated overlays, modal dialogues, floating control panels, and interactive hover states.
- **Surface Active / Border Subdued (`#1e3a5f`):** Precision containment lines and contextual dividers.

### Content & Accents
- **Primary Text (`#ffffff`):** Crisp pure white reserved for key metrics, headline anchors, and primary CTA text.
- **Secondary Text (`#cbd5e1`):** Slate off-white for body copy, analytical labels, metadata, and supporting metrics.
- **Muted Text (`#64748b`):** Subdued technical information, timestamps, and deactivated states.
- **Primary Accent (`#60a5fa`):** Soft electric icy blue for focus indicators, vital transactional trajectories, and affirmative active states.
- **Secondary Accent (`#93c5fd`):** Lighter ice highlight used sparingly for hovered states, pill accents, and micro-interactions.

## Typography

Typography relies on **Hanken Grotesk** across all roles to ensure geometric purity, sharp numeric rendering, and institutional legibility.

### Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Numbers and Currency:** Always pair numeric financial displays with tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to align data across tables and feeds.
- **Tracking:** Maintain slight negative tracking on large headlines to reinforce structural solidarity. Micro-labels and table tags require subtle positive letter spacing for optimal readability at small sizes.
- **Hierarchy Enforcement:** Reserve bold weights strictly for key numeric readouts, section anchors, and interactive labels. Long-form context uses regular weights to maintain visual calmness.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built on an absolute 8px grid (with 4px sub-increments for micro-components), emphasizing dense, data-rich compositions without visual clutter.

### Layout Architecture
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fluid grid. Gutter: 24px (`1.5rem`), Max Container Width: 1440px. Optimized for side-by-side data visualization and transactional social feeds.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid. Gutter: 20px, Margin: 32px (`2rem`). Secondary analytical widgets collapse beneath primary feeds or order books.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4-column grid. Gutter: 16px (`1rem`), Margin: 16px (`1rem`). Full-width operational controls with anchored bottom action zones.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through **tonal surface stepping** and **low-contrast outlines**, avoiding standard blurred drop shadows that degrade legibility in dark environments.

### Surface Stratification
1. **Floor (`#060d17`):** Primary viewport backdrop.
2. **Base Cards (`#0b192c`):** Positioned directly on the canvas, delineated by a hairline perimeter border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`.
3. **Elevated Panels & Flyouts (`#0f2238`):** Positioned over base cards, using `1px solid rgba(96, 165, 250, 0.2)` combined with an ambient, deep-tinted shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(2, 6, 12, 0.7)`.
4. **Focused Modals & Popovers:** Elevated using `#0f2238` with an inner hairline highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`) and border `1px solid #60a5fa`.

## Shapes

The design uses controlled, architectural corner radii. Shapes emphasize precision engineering rather than playful bubbles.

- **Base Components (Inputs, Small Buttons, Badges):** Standardized at `rounded-md` (`0.25rem` / 4px).
- **Cards, Modules, and Modals:** Standardized at `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- **Pills and Indicators:** Strictly confined to status indicators, live market tags, and transactional badges (`rounded-full`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#60a5fa`, label `#060d17` (bold weight). Hover: `#93c5fd`. Border radius: `0.375rem`. Focus ring: `2px solid #93c5fd` with 2px offset in `#060d17`.
- **Secondary / Institutional:** Background `#0f2238`, label `#ffffff`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`. Hover: border `1px solid #60a5fa`, background `#1e3a5f`.
- **Ghost:** Text `#cbd5e1`, transparent background. Hover: background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`, text `#ffffff`.

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Container:** Background `#0b192c`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, height 42px, radius `0.375rem`.
- **Typography:** Value in `#ffffff`, placeholder in `#64748b`.
- **Active / Focus:** Border `1px solid #60a5fa`, subtle inner glow `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #60a5fa`.

### Cards & Modules
- **Structure:** Background `#0b192c`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`, radius `0.5rem`, padding `1.5rem`.
- **Header Pattern:** Border bottom `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, space below `1rem`. Action items positioned top-right.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- **Row Architecture:** Alternating transparent backgrounds with active hover states turning to `#0f2238`.
- **Dividers:** Subtle line `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.
- **Data Display:** Primary metrics in `#ffffff` (tabular figures), metadata in `#cbd5e1`, auxiliary details in `#64748b`.

### Checkboxes & Switches
- **Checkboxes:** Square with `0.25rem` radius, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. Selected state fills with `#60a5fa` and displays crisp `#060d17` checkmark.
- **Switches:** Track `#0f2238` with border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`. Handle `#ffffff`. Active track `#60a5fa` with `#060d17` handle.

### Chips & Badges
- **Market / Metric Badges:** Background `rgba(96, 165, 250, 0.1)`, text `#60a5fa`, border `1px solid rgba(96, 165, 250, 0.3)`, radius `0.25rem`, padding `0.25rem 0.5rem`.
- **Neutral Tags:** Background `rgba(203, 213, 225, 0.05)`, text `#cbd5e1`, border `1px solid rgba(203, 213, 225, 0.1)`.

### Specialized Domain Components
- **Order Books / Commerce Bids:** Dense, tabular lines featuring proportional horizontal fill bars indicating liquidity depth using `rgba(96, 165, 250, 0.08)`.
- **Verified Trader / Merchant Badge:** Monochromatic slate-gray ring enclosing a crisp white star/check icon, avoiding loud multi-colored trust marks.
